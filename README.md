# Solideon

> Hardware at the speed of software.

Solideon builds autonomous, deployable factories for complex hardware. We combine industrial robotics with proprietary software to plan, manufacture, assemble, and inspect entire structures and vehicles without human intervention.

## Our mission

We are decentralizing manufacturing so critical hardware can be built close to where it is needed. Production should not have to wait months for tooling or depend on a single, distant supply chain.

Our systems serve aerospace, maritime, and national security programs today. Long term, we want humanity to manufacture wherever it goes, on Earth and beyond.

## What we do

- Turn validated 3D models into robot-ready manufacturing plans
- Coordinate robot motion, welding, and in-process sensing for wire arc additive manufacturing
- Build modular microfactories that can deploy where the work happens
- Connect additive manufacturing, machining, assembly, and inspection in one workflow
- Help teams produce large structures without dedicated tooling or a distant factory
